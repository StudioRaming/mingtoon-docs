0.1.7 Hotfix

0.1.7 hotfix · the package version stays 0.1.7

How to read a hotfix

A hotfix is the same 0.1.7 package uploaded again without a version bump. Download it again from BOOTH and overwrite Assets/StudioRaming/MingToon. To see which round your package contains, check the entries in Docs/PATCH_NOTES_0.1.7.md inside the package. New rounds are always added at the top of this page.

Hotfix 1 — 2026-09-07 (round 1)​

After updating

  • The face proxy needs nothing beyond the overwrite. Open the scene and the editable material's proxy normal is computed by the shader right away. No re-upload is needed.
  • A material converted from a lilToon source with no outline that relies on stencil needs a re-conversion or the Outline > Stencil Settings matched to the surface values by hand.
  • A material with Enable Emission on needs a re-conversion or one press of Sync Fallback Properties in the Manager, then a re-upload, before its VRChat fallback turns black.
  • A project whose VRChat build was stuck on Material recovery is incomplete cleans itself up after one editor restart following the overwrite.
  • lilToon-converted materials that rely on stencil (eye overlays, hidden-mesh masks) need a re-conversion or the stencil values set back by hand.
  • No re-upload is needed.

Face proxy normals kept "coming undone" in the scene​

  • Symptom — a tuned proxy snapped back to the original normals at every scene save, Play mode, reload or validation, and meanwhile the inspector pointed at a preview clone so edits never reached the source material.
  • Fix — the temporary UV7 preview mesh and material swap is gone. On the editable material the shader computes the proxy normal itself. The upload copy is still baked from the proxy settings as before, and that material compiles the live path out. Live and baked use the same math.
  • Also fixed — on a Skinned Mesh Renderer with a Root Bone the Proxy Area Mask center landed off the face. The shader's object space there is the root bone's (usually Hips), so the editor now publishes a per-renderer correction.

Converting a lilToon material with no outline left its outline ignoring the stencil mask​

  • Symptom — an eye stencilled to show through the fringe showed through on the surface while its outline stayed hidden behind the hair. lilToon variants without an outline carry unused outline stencil values, and the outline MingToon draws after conversion received that neutral unit.
  • Fix — when the source draws no outline, the outline inherits the surface stencil unit. A source that draws its own outline keeps its own values as before.

The VRChat fallback shader painted the whole avatar white with the emission colour​

  • Symptom — an avatar dropped to the fallback by a shader block or a failed shader load came out white with only its outline left. The fallback _EmissionColor carried the tint × intensity of any material with Enable Emission on, and the VRChat fallback shaders add that colour over the whole surface when no emission map is set.
  • Fix — the fallback emission alias is now always black. MingToon's own emission is unchanged.

Stale NDMF clone records kept blocking the VRChat build​

  • Symptom — after one build of an avatar that uses NDMF, every later VRChat build failed with Material recovery is incomplete.
  • Fix — recovery records for the clone materials NDMF creates under Packages/nadena.dev.ndmf/__Generated/ and deletes after the build stayed in the journal. The missing-material check now looks at the file on disk instead of the GUID mapping alone, and such records are cleared at the next recovery with a single warning.

Look presets overwrote the stencil values​

  • Symptom — the lilToon conversion carries the source stencil unit across (Ref, Comp, Pass and so on), but the factory look applied right after it held the shader defaults (Ref 0, Always, Keep), so the stencil that eye overlays and hidden-mesh masks rely on went back to 0. Re-applying a look did the same.
  • Fix — both stencil units (surface and outline, 14 properties) are now neither captured into nor written by a preset.
