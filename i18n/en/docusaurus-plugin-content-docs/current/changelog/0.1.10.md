*Open beta · 14 September 2026*

## In one line {#v0110}

The face proxy gained a plane, and texture and layer editing moved into cards.

:::warning[Check before updating]
- Backup: back up the whole project before installing. Installing into a new project is fine too.
- Re-bake: restore materials you already baked to editable, then bake them again.
- Re-upload: VRChat avatars need uploading again to receive this.
- Look changes: outline brightness and color, the face proxy, emission blending.
- Every texture in the project may be reimported once on the first update.
:::

## New features

- **Plane** — added to the face proxy shapes. It flattens face shading to a front-facing basis.
- **Overall Saturation** · **Overall Emission Intensity** — adjust the whole character at once from Master Adjust.
- **Rim Shade Color** — sets the rim shade's color separately. At a blend of 0 it uses the existing shadow color.
- **Follow Base Map** · **Follow Alpha** — the outline follows the surface's color and its transparency separately.
- **Bulk Import Settings** — pick a maximum resolution and compression quality per texture type and apply them.
- **Mask Details** — adjust contrast, blur and input levels, plus hue, saturation, brightness and gamma.
- **Mask Channel** — choose `R / G / B / A / Luminance` with buttons. Matcap masks also have `RGB`.
- Add, move and delete buttons were added to the texture, normal and matcap layers.
- A layer can be turned off instead of deleted. Turn it back on and it uses the settings it had stored.
- The texture list sorts by section, size, resolution and name.
- Choose the bake method between `FastUpload` and `Legacy GPU Precision Optimization`.
- Inspector cards, spacing and section headers were tidied so they read at narrow widths.

## Changed defaults and look changes

- **Receiver Depth Pushback** moved from the Depth Shadow (formerly 2D Shadow) tab to face shading.
- With Depth Shadow on and the pushback above 0, that material does not receive projected shadows.
- Set the pushback back to 0, or turn Depth Shadow off, and projected shadows apply again.
- Complex lighting calculations were taken out of the outline. Check line brightness and color on existing materials.
- The existing sphere, cylinder and capsule proxies are now marked as experimental shapes. The plane is recommended.
- Emission blending now follows lilToon. Existing Hue, Color and Overlay values are handled as Add.
- The six factory presets were updated. Reapplying them overwrites the values.
  **Basic Toon High/Low** · **Hard Toon High/Low** · **Soft Toon High/Low**.

## Fixed

- Fixed: the color preset also overwrote the shadow color map's UV settings.
- Fixed: the matcap mask dropped out as the camera moved away, throwing off the texture bindings.
- Fixed: labels on the row below the mask box were clipped to two or three characters.
- Fixed: the move-up button stayed in the previous language after the inspector language was changed.

## Known limitations

- Depth effects depend on the depth information the world, the camera and mirrors provide.
- This release is the BRP core. The URP add-on and Ming Light Controller are not included.
- The first bake and the first compile take longer because the cache is empty.
