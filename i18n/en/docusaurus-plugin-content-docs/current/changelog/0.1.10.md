*Open Beta · September 14, 2026*

## 0.1.10 {#v0110}

:::warning[Please read before installing or updating]

- **Full texture reimport:** When updating to 0.1.10 for the first time, the changed import validation process may trigger a **one-time reimport of every texture in your project.** This is not designed to repeat with every update. Please report it if it continues after the update has completed.
- **Protect your existing work:** We recommend **backing up your entire project before installing**, or **installing and using MingToon in a new Unity project**.
- **Open Beta changes:** Updates during the beta may **change the look (colors, lighting, etc.) and presets**, and **add or remove features**. Check the results before applying an update to existing work, as the appearance may change.

:::

**Shader and bake processing, the inspector, and texture and layer editing have all been improved.** A planar mode was added to the face proxy, and face-shadow adjustment, lilToon conversion, and build processing were reorganized. Factory presets were updated as well.

This release is the **BRP core**. It supports Unity 2021.3 / 2022.3 LTS. The URP add-on and Ming Light Controller are not included. The existing Open Beta terms remain unchanged.

### Shader and bake optimization

- **Less repeated work** — Reusable lighting, normal, and tangent calculations are shared during baking, while unused static calculations and shader paths are reduced.
- **Lighter outline path** — Complex outline lighting and unnecessary branches were simplified. Check outline brightness and color on existing materials after updating.
- **Separate bake modes** — Choose `FastUpload` or `Legacy GPU Precision` separately for build/upload and manual bake. Select the same mode in both places when comparing the editor preview with the uploaded result.
- **Better reuse of generated results** — Generated results are reused when material, texture, mesh, and animation inputs are the same, reducing accidental reuse of stale results when inputs differ.
- **More reliable restoration** — Restoration of editable materials, keywords, and meshes after a build or bake, including cancellation and failure recovery, was strengthened.

The improvement varies with material composition, rendering conditions, and cache state. This release does not turn the change into a fixed FPS or GPU improvement rate that applies to every avatar.

### Inspector and presets

- **Consistent layout** — Section headers, the top navigation area, cards, buttons, and spacing were reorganized to improve readability in a narrow inspector.
- **Clearer performance cost display** — Cost indicators in the top and sticky areas now use a consistent presentation, with more compact gauges, percentages, and details. They are estimated costs for the selected features, not actual GPU occupancy.
- **Presets and color organization** — Quick application and save/management areas now follow the same layout. A problem where color presets also overwrote the Shadow Color Map UV setting was fixed.
- **Master adjustments** — Use `Overall Saturation` and `Overall Emission Strength` to adjust the related output in one place.
- **Rim Shade color** — The Rim Shade color can be adjusted independently. At zero separate-color blending, it uses the existing shadow color.
- **Factory preset update** — Common, Face, and Skin factory presets for Basic, Hard, and Soft Toon were updated, and the High/Low display was organized.
- **Labels and tooltips** — Korean, English, and Japanese display strings, long labels, and duplicated descriptions were cleaned up.

### Texture and mask cards

- **Texture list organization** — Textures can be grouped by section or sorted by GPU memory, resolution, or name. The expand-details button is placed at the far right of the card.
- **Batch import settings** — Choose Max Size, PC format, and compression quality by type for main, normal, AO, MatCap, mask, and alpha-mask textures. This changes the original textures' import settings, so it also affects other materials that share the same texture.
- **Quick large preview** — Rest the pointer on a texture for about 0.3 seconds to open a large preview. The preview closes when the pointer leaves.
- **Better texture browsing** — Folders indicate whether they contain textures, and folders with textures are ordered so they are easier to find.
- **X/Y linking** — Tiling and Offset have X/Y link buttons and a wider drag area for placement controls.
- **Edit inside cards** — Previews, color and blending, UV, channels, and adjustment controls are grouped inside cards, reducing repeated rows.
- **Unified mask cards** — Mask previews, strength, UV, inversion, channels, and detailed settings can be adjusted in one place.
- **Mask adjustment** — Supported slots expose Contrast, Blur, Input Levels, Hue, Saturation, Value, and Gamma. Input Levels can be changed with bar handles or numeric fields.
- **Channel selection** — Choose `R / G / B / A / Luma` with buttons. MatCap masks also offer `RGB`.
- **Card reset** — The card header can reset its connected texture, color, and blend settings together.
- **AO card organization** — Edit `AO (Occlusion)` and its strength in the texture card, then adjust Contrast, Blur, and Input Levels in the card details. The result still depends on the authored map and material settings.

### Layer editing

- **Add, move, and delete** — Texture, normal, and MatCap layers have add controls plus individual move and delete controls.
- **Turn off while keeping values** — A layer can be disabled and re-enabled without deleting it. When enabled again, it uses its saved texture, color, and blend settings.
- **Animation links** — Connected animations are checked before a layer is moved or deleted, and matching curves are handled with the edit. Material and curve changes can be undone together. Review the guidance when a link cannot be confirmed or when the layer count itself is animated.
- **Color-only layers** — Color-only layers that have no texture but use color and masks are handled by the conversion path.

### Face proxy and shadows

- **Planar proxy added** — A `Plane` option was added as the default face-proxy shape. Use it when aligning face-area shading to a flat, front-facing reference.
- **Experimental shapes marked** — The existing sphere, cylinder, and capsule shapes remain available as experimental shapes.
- **Related controls grouped** — The proxy shape, normal strength, normal-map influence, and related settings are placed closer together.
- **Receiver pushback moved** — `Receiver Depth Pushback` was moved from 2D Shadow to Face Shading. When 2D Shadow is enabled, it pushes the recorded depth and receiver surface back together to reduce depth interference from the face itself.
- **Cast-shadow behavior documented** — When 2D Shadow is enabled and pushback is positive, cast-shadow receiving is disabled on that material. Turning 2D Shadow off or returning pushback to 0 applies the original cast-shadow setting again.
- **Experimental depth controls** — Depth-record exclusion and receiver offset are marked as experimental. They can affect ordinary projected shadows and world post-process effects, so use them only when needed.

### Outline color and alpha

- **Follow the base map** — The color resulting from the base map and texture-layer composite can be used for the outline.
- **Follow alpha** — Following the color and following the surface alpha are separate choices. Outline color and transparency can be adjusted independently.

### lilToon conversion

- **Keep values from disabled features** — Supported values from Backlight, Rim, Glitter, Outline, Reflection, Toon Specular, MatCap, and Emission are transferred more broadly while their enabled states remain distinct.
- **Emission blending** — The four Normal, Add, Screen, and Multiply blend modes and their default now match lilToon. Existing Hue, Color, and Overlay values are handled as Add.
- **Reflection, rim, and shadow improvements** — Reflection-module activation, shadow border color, Rim Shade color, Fresnel Rim range, and MatCap mask and UV handling were improved.
- **Color-only inputs** — Surface, emission, and MatCap inputs expressed only through color without a texture can also be converted.
- **Missing and loss guidance** — Materials referenced only by animation and settings without an exact counterpart are shown in the conversion report. Review the report and the rendered result after conversion.

### Depth Light and WARUDO

- **Depth Light management** — Creation, reuse, removal, and temporary build-time handling of MingToon-owned Depth Lights were reorganized.
- **WARUDO UMod build** — The build now optimizes within the exported prefab scope, connects generated shaders and textures to the bundle, and restores authored materials afterward.
- **Prevent incomplete results** — A failure to register generated assets or restore materials is reported as a build error.

Depth effects depend on depth information supplied by the world, camera, and mirror. This change does not guarantee post-processing or bloom behavior in every world.

### What to check after updating

1. Back up the project before updating. Update through VCC for the VCC installation path, and through the MingToon update window for the DLL installation path.
2. Materials that were already baked must be **restored to editable materials and baked again** for these changes to apply. Re-upload avatars already uploaded to VRChat.
3. Reapplying a factory preset overwrites values in the selected scope. Apply only the presets you need to materials with manual adjustments.
4. Check lighting, outlines, face proxies, and emission blending on materials that use them. If you use pushback, also check its cast-shadow receiving condition.
5. The first bake or compile can take longer because the cache and generated-result rules changed. Later time also varies with the material and cache state.
