*Open beta · 7 September 2026*

## In one line

Depth Shadow was rebuilt, and property names and the add-on lineup were tidied up.

:::warning[Check before updating]
- Upgrade path: delete the `Assets/StudioRaming/MingToon` folder and import fresh.
- Move files you made yourself out of the folder first. Do not delete `MingLightController`.
- Values stored on 0.1.6 materials do not carry over. Set them again from the new defaults.
- Animation: the `_Ming` prefix was dropped from property IDs. Reconnect your curves.
- Do not strip the prefix in bulk. Some names kept it, such as `_MingVirtualLight*`.
- Re-bake: bake outputs are invalidated. Bake again and re-upload the avatar.
- Look changes: face shading, shadow shape, additional light brightness, Depth Rim Light.
- Four things were split out and sold separately.
Face SDF Studio, Mask Maker, Ming Light Controller and the URP backend.
:::

## New features

- **Enable Emission Layer 2** — a second emission map gives each part its own color. Off by default.
- **Map Alpha Masks Intensity** — uses the emission map's alpha as an intensity mask. It is on both layers.
- **Quick Settings** — the items that act on the whole material at once are gathered at the top of the inspector.
- **Two-Pass Mode** (formerly Two-Sided Dual Pass) — draws the far side of a two-sided surface before the near side.
- The sawtooth where a skirt flap folds disappears. It is BRP only, so the row is hidden on URP materials.
- **Transparent Depth Prepass** — stops the outline punching through the surface in `Transparent (3000)`.
- **Enable Distance Fade** · **Enable Fresnel Alpha** — make a surface fade out by distance and by angle.
- **Gradation LUT** — remaps the base map's color through a per-channel ramp.
- **Hue Rotation Space** — `OKLab` rotates the hue alone while holding brightness and vividness.
- **Performance Distance (m)** · **Performance Distance Scale** — turn heavy modules off entirely at distance.
- **Re-bake meshes now** — leaves the look values alone and re-bakes only the mesh channels you enabled.
- **Backlight Blend Mode** · **Front Light Blend Mode** — pick from seven ways of mixing the color. `Blend Opacity`, added alongside them, only reduces presence. The defaults are `Add` and 1.
- **Backlit Suppression Offset** — pushes the point where the cast shadow disappears towards the dark side. Default 0.25.
- Copy and paste buttons for material settings were added at the top of the inspector. Undo works on them.
- The Manager now has five tabs, `Overview` · `Setup` · `Look & Bake` · `Upload` · `Optimize`. The `Analyze` button in `Optimize` shows four suggestions for this avatar.
- The `?` button on a section header opens the documentation page for the inspector language.

## Changed defaults and look changes

- Depth Shadow (formerly 2D Shadow) was rebuilt around a single silhouette.
- The shadow lands further away in proportion to how far the front object floats above the receiver, so depth reads.
- The tab was tidied down to ten items.
  **Shadow Width** · **Shadow Intensity** · **Depth Stretch** · **Depth Curve** · **Depth Falloff**.
  **Softness** · **Blur** · **Depth Shadow Bias** · **Receiver Depth Pushback** · **Suppress Backlit Silhouette**.
- **Depth Shadow Bias** changed scale by a factor of 10. 1 is now 10 cm.
- **Depth Blur** and **Light Pushback** are gone. Their replacements are listed in [Depth Shadow](/guides/depth-effects#2d-그림자).
- The more the camera looks down, the shorter **Depth Stretch** gets. There is no control; it is automatic.
- **Additional Light Energy Cap** is new. Its default of 0.35 holds point and spot lights down.
- **Added Light Brightness Maximum** is now the absolute ceiling on the final output peak. Stored numbers mean something different now.
- **SSAO Radius** runs 0.001 to 0.03 with a default of 0.005. The explanation is in [SSAO](/guides/depth-effects#ssao-화면-공간-차폐).
- **Contact Falloff** is now a multiple of **SSAO Radius** (0.25 to 4, default 1) rather than meters.
- Fade distances merged into **Shared Fade Distance** (default 200). Do not carry the old numbers across unchanged.
- Display names were unified on Depth Rim Light (formerly 2D Depth Rim). **Scene Light Influence** was added, defaulting to 1.
- Defaults changed on nine lighting items, and **Enable Translucency** is now off by default.
- **Surface Mode** names now carry the render queue.
  Opaque (2000) · Cutout (2450) · Semi-Transparent (2499) · Transparent (3000).
- On Unified Shadow materials, projected shadows are brighter than before in scenes with ambient light.
- **Projection Feather Radius** is now in world width units, 0 to 20 (default 3.5). It converts automatically when you open the material.
- **Cast Edge Feather Quality** was cut down to three.
  Light3Tap · Standard5Tap · High9Tap.
- The factory looks' **Rim Light** color purity default dropped from 10 to 1.
- Materials with **Enable Fresnel Rim** on show a different edge color in scenes with additional lights.
- Fresnel Rim being applied twice in URP was brought in line with BRP.
- The top-level **Rim** group was renamed **Stylized Lighting**.
- The MingToon menu moved to the top level, `StudioRaming > MingToon`.
- The **Face Proxy Depth** and **VRChat Preview** menus were removed. There is no replacement.

:::note[The SSAO Radius range widened again after this]
The 0.001 to 0.03 above is the range as of 0.1.7.
In the current version **SSAO Radius** runs 0.001 to 0.05 m.
The explanation is in [Depth-based effects](/guides/depth-effects#ssao-화면-공간-차폐).
:::

## Fixed

- Fixed: the face shadow snapped and jumped as light crossed the center of the face.
- Fixed: a depth offset meant for the face alone pushed the body out too.
- Fixed: face proxy normals flipped or reacted too narrowly under front light. The scene view preview failing to build was fixed with it.
- Fixed: turning on `Gradation LUT` did nothing.
- Fixed: deleting the bake outputs and baking again required restarting the editor.
- Fixed: `Channel Source` and `Invert Source Colors` could not be chosen on the second emission slot.
- Fixed: in lilToon conversion the emission channel setting stayed at its pre-conversion value.
- Fixed: the build shader failed to compile on alpha and region mask materials.
- That also removes the roughly 5 extra minutes of upload caused by the editable shader riding along on the avatar.
- Avatars already uploaded need re-uploading to receive this fix.
- Fixed: `Startup recovery is incomplete` repeated on every editor launch.
- Fixed: narrowing the inspector clipped the slider and the number field so values could not be entered.
- Fixed: the text on the preset scope toggles was clipped and unreadable. The revert button appearing outside the item box was fixed too.
- Fixed: opening Face SDF Studio first pinned the inspector to Korean as well.
- Fixed: `Render Disabled` could not turn off the transparent depth prepass.
- Fixed: creating a look preset from a prefab also saved changes across the whole project.
- Fixed: a region applied once in the `One Slot / Tiled Regions` window could not be deleted.

## Known limitations

- Materials that already had `Gradation LUT` on will look different. Turn it off if you need the old image.
- Fur, gem and refraction conversion, and automatic rewriting of old material curves, are not in this release.
- Depth light behavior is measured in the editor. It was not verified in the VRChat client.
- Two precision fixes were confirmed by shader compilation only. There was no on-device render verification.
- The `One Slot / Tiled Regions` window does not read the material's current values. Set the configuration before applying.
- Three public properties on `MingToonManager` were deleted.
  `UseModularAvatarVrcIntegration` · `VrcFullExpressionMenu` · `VrcMenuAtExpressionsRoot`.
- A user script referencing these names produces a compile error. Remove the reference.
