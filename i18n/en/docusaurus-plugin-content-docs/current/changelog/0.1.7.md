*Closed Beta · 2026-08-22*

0.1.7 is the cumulative update since 0.1.6. It brings together improvements to face authoring, shadows and SSAO, VRChat runtime control, conversion and bake reliability, the inspector, and MingToon Manager.

## Check Before Upgrading

:::caution[Generated shaders and mesh-bake caches will be rebuilt]
The generator and bake-input contracts have changed. They will be regenerated automatically on the next build, so the first build may take longer than usual. Shaders are included in avatar asset bundles, so **VRChat avatars that have already been uploaded must be uploaded again to receive the 0.1.7 fixes and optimizations.**
:::

:::caution[If you previously baked face normals into UV7, return them to Live]
Starting with 0.1.7, face-normal pressing is calculated live by the shader while editing and automatically baked only into the VRChat upload copy. Run `Return Face Normals to Live` once in MingToon Manager. If a material with legacy tangent normals in UV7 selects Face SDF UV7, `Face Map Coordinates` is also returned to Base UV to prevent those coordinates from being misread. This operation supports Undo.
:::

:::caution[Shadows and SSAO may look different]

- `2D Shadow Fade Distance` now defaults to 12 m. It begins fading at 9 m and disappears at 12 m. Set it to 0 for unlimited distance like before.
- The actual range of `Projection Feather Radius` is 0–48 px. Stored values above 48 are clamped to 48 the next time the slider is changed.
- SSAO radius now defaults to 0.005 and is capped at 0.01. Edges and self-surface marks are handled more smoothly.
- The cast-band position and compositing order at shadow boundaries changed. Materials that use boundaries may show a clearer band near the shadow core.
:::

:::caution[Some face-cast controls were removed]
`Uniform Face Cast` and `Face Cast Stabilization` were removed because they did not produce a valid effect. Materials that used them return to the default cast-receive behavior.
:::

:::caution[Reinstall VRChat Expressions]
The final 0.1.7 contract uses **22 synced parameters and 120 bits**. Quality tiers and a Shadow Projection toggle were added, and the menu structure and placement workflow changed. Update the existing installation in MingToon Manager, then verify it with Gesture Manager.
:::

:::caution[New lighting defaults apply only to new materials and factory presets]
Nine defaults—including `Scene Light Color Influence`, `Indirect Light Compensation`, and `Additional Light PBR / Toon Blend`—were adjusted for production setups, and `2D Translucency` now defaults to off. Existing values stored in materials do not change. The new values are applied only when you reapply a factory preset or restore defaults.
:::

:::caution[Face SDF Studio and Mask Maker are separate products]
These two tools are not bundled with MingToon. When installed, they integrate with the inspector and MingToon Manager; otherwise, only installation guidance is shown. MingToon shaders and core authoring features continue to work normally.
:::

## New Features

### Face Authoring and Depth

- **Live face-normal pressing** — changing the proxy center, radius, shape, height, normal mode, or strength updates SceneView and GameView immediately. During upload, only the upload copy is baked; the scene original is untouched.
- **Sphere, Cylinder, and Capsule face proxies** — use one proxy to align both normal direction and the face region. Capsule height and axis can also be edited with the scene tool.
- **Face Proxy Depth (Experimental)** — writes a proxy volume instead of the actual facial relief to camera depth and, optionally, ShadowCaster. This reduces world-space SSAO around the nose and brow and facial self-shadow acne. It is off by default.
- **Face projected-shadow fallback on screens without depth** — explicitly opted-in materials can keep the face-shadow texture path even when no depth texture is available.
- **Near-clip protection** — reduces exposure of the inside of the head when the camera moves too close to the face.

### Shadows, SSAO, and Rim

- **1 / 2 / 4-tap 2D rim sample quality** — control quality and cost per material or through a VRC quality tier.
- **2D shadow fade distance** — defaults to 12 m, with fewer taps at long distances. False detections at screen edges and discontinuities between nearby separate meshes were also reduced.
- **Opt-in depth-effects runtime switch** — VRC FX can actually disable all depth effects and skip their sample cost.
- **Opt-in Shadow Projection runtime switch** — toggle projected shadows independently from the VRC quality menu.
- **Edge-rim group master, color, intensity, and cap** — adjust silhouette effects such as 2D rim, Fresnel rim, and backlight together, with a separate HDR peak limit.
- **`Visible in Shadow` for inner reflection and SSAO** — control how much of each effect remains inside existing shadows. This gate does not apply to point/spot additional-light reflections in WARUDO Built-in.
- **Glitter MatCap limit** — use a MatCap texture to mask view-dependent glitter regions.

### Detail and Output

- **Emission Layer 2** — combine independent maps, colors, and strengths to create region-specific emission.
- **Emission-map alpha strength mask** — both emission layers can use map alpha as per-pixel strength, with no additional texture sample.
- **Emission time animation** — configure per-layer scrolling, flashing, and darkness-driven emission.
- **Five MatCap slots** — supports per-slot rotation, circle mask, and emission contribution, plus shared MatCap normal-map strength.
- **Render Disabled** — removes every pass for a material, eliminating the draw call itself. Because this is a material pass state, use a Renderer or GameObject for runtime switching.

### Inspector and MingToon Manager

- **Copy and paste material settings** — five modes: `All`, `Values Only`, `Textures Only`, `Keywords Only`, and `Render Queue Only`, all with Undo.
- **Sticky toolbar and section navigation** — the top tools remain while scrolling, and a color bar jumps directly to each workflow group.
- **Collapse All / Expand All / Changed Only** — `Changed Only` keeps rows that differ from shader defaults and automatically opens their sections.
- **Quick Settings and material-cost summary** — inspect commonly used values and the estimated post-bake cost at the top of the inspector.
- **Streamlined material-role assignment** — in MingToon Manager stage 1, assign Face / Skin / Common per renderer and material slot, then immediately review which slots receive the converted look.
- **Four-stage MingToon Manager workflow** — organized as `1 · Convert` → `2 · Setup` → `3 · Details` → `4 · Ship`, with the current task and reasons for deferral shown in each stage.
- **Select Face Materials Only** — select only Face materials before editing the face proxy or applying face-wide changes.
- **Upload texture resolution caps** — set maximum resolutions by slot type, including base, normal, and mask. Only upload copies are reduced; source textures are unchanged.

### Conversion and VRChat

- **Convert pink Missing Shader materials** — reads stored properties to recover and convert NiloToon, lilToon, and Standard-family materials.
- **Full render-state preservation** — conversion retains stencil, render queue, Cull, and transparency modes including Cutout, Fade, and Premultiply.
- **VRC quality tiers: Low / Mid / High** — High preserves authored values, Mid caps sample counts, and Low lowers sample counts further while disabling the Depth Effects Master and projected-shadow feathering.
- **Extended VRC Light Volumes support** — supports volume specular, point-light shadows, normal bias, and strength in addition to indirect light. Required variants are enabled automatically for avatar uploads; world builds enable them only when Light Volumes are present in the scene.
- **Direct Expressions-root placement** — place MingToon Controls in a subfolder or directly at the Expressions root, and flatten menus to avoid the eight-control limit.

## Changes

- **Inspector information architecture reorganized** — `Lighting` moved under Basic Setup, while `Shadow` became its own group. PBR Surface, Toon Specular, and Region Masks now form the `PBR` group, and depth sections use result-oriented names.
- **Simple mode redefined** — it now keeps only the controls required for initial setup. Advanced diagnostics and internal technical information are hidden from release builds. Variant dump/record tools and the Depth Debug Post Process appear only in `MINGTOON_DEV` builds.
- **Additional-light cap semantics changed** — the cap now limits HDR peaks directly instead of multiplying the base color. This prevents runaway multi-light output without erasing specular on dark materials.
- **Shadow boundary redesigned** — the cast band fills from the lit side and peaks immediately before the shadow core. It is composited last over all shadows and SSAO, but before additive lights, and floating contours inside the combined shadow were removed.
- **SSAO compositing changed** — SSAO color applies to total SSAO coverage instead of only the increment over existing shadows. Its color and brightness settings remain visible over form shadows and backlighting.
- **Scope of `Overall Effect` and `Depth Effects Master` clarified** — disabling the depth master now disables SSAO as well.
- **Projection feather range is 0–48 px** — the UI range now matches the maximum actually available to quality tiers.
- **Color purity cap increased to 10** — expanded from 2 for stronger color stylization.
- **`Uniform Face Cast` and `Face Cast Stabilization` removed** — nonfunctional properties and preset seeds were deleted.

## Improvements

- **MatCap upload optimization** — Built-in now folds fixed values for MatCap slots 2–5 into constants. In a five-slot measurement, fragment instructions fell from 1,286 to 117, nearly eliminating the cost gap with URP.
- **Bake-cache correctness** — cache keys now include proxy shape, axis, height, region mask, UV ownership, and upload-only paths. Outputs from different characters or editor bakes are no longer overwritten.
- **Upload restoration reliability** — renderer and material restoration is isolated per entry and saves only changed assets. One failed entry no longer blocks the remaining restoration or saves unrelated work.
- **Preset preservation scope** — presets preserve material identity and character-specific placement, including Surface Mode, Cutout, render queue, and face proxy.
- **Mask Maker integration** — shows the affected region while painting, and mode switching and Undo retain the editing state.
- **Shader warming and keyword recovery** — warms only the active backend and actual pass keywords, then resumes from the interruption point. Detailed maintainer diagnostics remain available only in development builds.

## Fixes

- Face SDF left/right selection snapping abruptly as the light crossed the exact center.
- Face-region vertex masks being ignored by ShadowCaster and Depth passes, causing the body to receive the face offset.
- Upload baking mistaking a texture face-region mask for the entire face. These materials now stay on the Live path to preserve the same result.
- Multiple Face slots on one renderer being baked with the first slot's values despite using different proxies or runtime frames. Mismatched slots now safely remain Live.
- The proxy scene tool ignoring parent or renderer scale, offsetting click positions.
- AlphaToCoverage Cutout ForwardAdd producing a bright fringe under additional lights.
- VRChat mirrors reading another camera's depth or depth lights not actually being enabled.
- 2D shadows breaking across nearby separate meshes and producing false detections at screen edges.
- SSAO self-occluding sloped surfaces and producing square marks or stepped boundaries at zero bias.
- lilToon conversion not enabling PBR, emission, layer, outline, and alpha-mask toggles.
- Missing Shader and standard conversions losing parts of the Surface state.
- Look presets reverting alpha modes and face-proxy values.
- Shared-mesh baking, UV-channel conflicts, parts without tangents, and interrupted rebake or restore operations.
- Failed rollback after reimporting a vertex-paint target FBX with Read/Write enabled restoring a stale Mesh reference.
- Multi-root face-proxy editing, pending-edit cleanup, and multi-selection state keys colliding.
- Narrow-inspector clipping and flicker, sticky-toolbar DPI coordinates, preset-toggle wrapping, and missing bulk-edit items.

## Documentation

- All 457 user-visible properties are placed in the Korean, English, and Japanese references, with zero unclassified entries.
- Inspector, MingToon Manager, face and SDF, shadow and SSAO, VRChat, conversion, and bake guides were updated for the final 0.1.7 behavior.
- Development diagnostics and obsolete bundled-product guidance that do not appear in release builds were removed.
