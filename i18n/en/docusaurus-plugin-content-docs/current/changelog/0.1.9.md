*Open Beta · September 9, 2026*

## 0.1.9 {#v019}

This update focuses on **choosing conversion looks and colors, the Manager workflow, bake stability, and generated texture optimization**.

### Manager and look and color selection

- **Work organized into three tabs** — The tabs are `Get Started`, `Look & Bake`, and `Optimize`. Select your conversion targets and convert them in Get Started, then adjust the look and bake settings of existing MingToon materials in Look & Bake. Optimize contains automatic processing and texture resolution limits. The material selection and target scope at the top are shared across all three tabs.
- **Choose looks and colors separately** — Added a Color Preset selector below Look Preset. The default is `Neutral`. The look is applied first, followed by the selected colors. This replaces the previous shadow-color overwrite checkbox.
- **Keep Existing Values** — Select `Keep Existing Values` in Color Preset to retain the colors converted from the source. Previous settings that preserved shadow colors carry over to this option. Selecting a separate color preset replaces the corresponding colors with that preset's values.
- **Preserve shadow bands** — Applying a look preset during conversion no longer overwrites the shadow-band strength, boundaries, width, or blending settings taken from the source. Color preset selection and preservation of band settings are handled separately.

- **Reapply to current MingToon materials** — When only editable MingToon materials remain and there are no materials to convert, Get Started can reapply the selected look and colors to all MingToon materials on the character. A confirmation explains what will be overwritten, and Undo restores the previous values.
- **Menus and components** — Internal development diagnostics are hidden from product menus, and the manually added product components are organized around MingToon Manager and Ming Light Controller. The project validation menu remains available.

### lilToon conversion

- **First and second shadow colors and strength** — Source shadow strength, boundaries, and softness are reflected in MingToon settings. The second shadow's alpha is combined with overall shadow strength, fixing cases where the second shadow remained at zero strength or became too faint at intermediate alpha values.
- **Additional shadow colors** — The source's first shadow color is passed to other shadow settings so they do not remain white even when shadow-color unification is off. A source second shadow with zero alpha does not enable the second band.
- **Convert the materials that can be processed** — If conversion fails for some materials, the remaining materials continue to be processed. Failed slots retain their originals, and successful conversion results are kept. Problems in postprocessing, such as applying the selected look or colors, also appear in the results so you can distinguish completed, excluded, and failed items.
- **Transparent materials** — For ordinary transparent materials, the visible-surface cutoff is separated from the cutoff used for depth processing, and animated source cutoffs are preserved more reliably. Refraction materials retain their existing separate conversion path.

Because the shadow calculations differ, not every lilToon look will match pixel for pixel. After conversion, check the actual appearance, including lighting and masks.

### Bake and conversion stability

- Fixed temporary baked shaders sometimes remaining on editable materials after VRChat SDK builds.
- Fixed cutout edges changing after baking on some alpha-masked materials, such as straps with no main texture assigned.
- **Materials turning white after baking** — Fixed an issue where emission that was turned off could be enabled during baking and cover the entire material in white. Each material's enabled or disabled state is preserved even when emission intensity or color is controlled by animation.
- **Emission turning off or changing state** — Fixed bake results so the enabled state of emission and the second emission layer is reflected correctly. Enabled layers are retained, and disabled layers are not turned on unexpectedly.
- **Glitter applied to other materials** — When converting or baking an avatar that uses Glitter on some materials, the effect could also be applied to materials with Glitter disabled. Each material now follows its own Glitter state.
- **Glitter mask stability** — Fixed an issue where a mask might not apply after a manual bake, or where region restriction might not work when the mask was enabled later. Mask inversion and color masks are also preserved.
- **Selective preset application** — After conversion or when reapplying a preset, the modules currently in use and their related settings are no longer overwritten by the preset's disabled values. The look is applied while preserving settings that must remain, rather than overwriting every preset value across the material.

### Performance and other improvements

- Improved baking to retain optimizations for fixed material settings and reduce redundant shader variants that do not affect the result.
- **Consolidated repeated shader calculations** — Organized repeated calculations in lighting direction and overall color adjustments, and reduced unnecessary processing for unused texture layers. The focus is on lowering rendering overhead while preserving the existing look and animation control.
- **Improved bake and upload preparation** — Reduced duplicate work while checking and saving material and keyword states. The improvement varies with the avatar's composition, used features, and shader cache state.
- **Distant MatCap display retained** — Fixed an issue where colors or emission produced by MatCap disappeared when the camera moved away.
- **Stronger save and recovery checks** — Improved the accuracy of checking material save and recovery state when saving failed or work was interrupted during baking.

### Texture optimization

- **Resolution limits on merged results** — Resolution limits apply to generated results after texture and normal layers are merged and masks are packed. Both regular masks and mask packs generated while editing materials follow the mask resolution setting.
- **Source import settings preserved** — Source textures' maximum size and compression settings are not changed to apply resolution limits or compression. `No limit` keeps the dimensions of the merged result.
- **Compression and saving of generated results** — Merged color and normal textures use compression for PC. Generated masks are stored losslessly to preserve channel values, with their dimensions reduced by the mask resolution limit.
- **Cache reuse and recovery** — Reduced unnecessary rewrites and imports when reusing generated results with the same settings. An existing cache is not reused unchanged when settings or output dimensions change. Files of unconfirmed origin at an output path are not overwritten or deleted without verification.
- **Generated folders on a fresh installation** — Mask output folders can now be created in projects installed from a package even when the `Assets/StudioRaming` folder does not exist.

To regenerate textures during automatic build and upload processing, check both `Optimize On Build / Upload (Applies To Everything)` and `Reviewed Texture Rewrites On Build (Opt-In)` in the Optimize tab. Size reduction and processing time will be confirmed after validation with actual avatars; no figures are provided in these notes yet.

### Animation analysis and editing stability

- **Exceptions during Animator analysis** — When reading invalid or unusual Animator states causes an exception, the failed analysis is reported and handled conservatively. This helps avoid fixing animated values in place when analysis is incomplete.
- **Scene view depth display recovery** — Improved how depth display is requested again for the current Scene view. Play Mode and depth-display suppression settings are preserved.

### Ming Light Controller 0.1.2

- **Fractional Int values and the final budget gate** — When the installed SDK accepts them, fractional Int defaults such as `0.5` are preserved, with a notice that integer consumers may floor or clamp them. If the final synced budget is exceeded, the build is blocked with the excess bit count and clear reduction guidance.
- **VRCFury boundary** — Full collaboration with VRCFury compression and a post-compression Inspector preview are deferred to the next version. These notes distinguish pre-compression estimates from what must be checked again during the build.

MLC is a separate package. To use it with MingToon 0.1.9, update MLC to 0.1.2 as well. Supported Unity versions are 2021.3 and 2022.3; VRChat uploads must use the Unity version required by the installed SDK.

### What to check after updating

- To apply the fixes to an already baked material, **restore it to an editable material and bake it again**. Avatars already uploaded to VRChat must be uploaded again.
- If you temporarily set emission intensity to 0 to avoid white rendering, adjust it to the desired value after updating and bake again.
- Values already changed by applying a preset in an earlier version are not restored automatically. Adjust the required settings again, or convert again from the original material and check the result.
